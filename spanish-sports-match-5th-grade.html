How to Play

  1. Each numbered tile hides a sports picture. Every picture is hiding under two tiles.
  2. A student picks two numbers.
  3. The teacher clicks those two tiles.
  4. When a tile turns over, everyone says the sport in Spanish.
  5. A match stays face up. If the pictures differ, the tiles flip back after about 2 seconds.
  6. Work together as a class to find all 6 pairs.

Words to say: fútbol, tenis, baloncesto, nadar, correr, saltar.
Need a hint? Turn on Show Words to see the Spanish word under each picture.
