How to Play

  1. Each number card hides a sports picture. Every picture is hiding under two cards.
  2. A student says two numbers in Spanish.
  3. The teacher clicks those two cards.
  4. A match stays face up. If the pictures differ, the cards flip back after about 2 seconds.
  5. Work together as a class to find all 10 pairs.

The numbers always stay in order, so number 12 is always in the same spot. Only the pictures behind them change in a new game.
